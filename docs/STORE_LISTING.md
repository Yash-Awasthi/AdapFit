**Full description** (under 4,000 characters):

> AdapFit tells you each morning whether to train as planned, go lighter,
> recover or rest, and shows you why. The answer comes from your own numbers
> compared with your own normal, not from an average person.
>
> **Your daily decision.** A 30-second check-in (sleep, soreness, energy, and
> HRV and resting heart rate if your watch records them) gives a recovery
> score against your personal baseline and one clear call for the day.
>
> **Workouts that adapt.** Sessions are built for how you recovered and what
> you have at home or in the gym. Log sets, reps and effort; after four weeks
> your training load (acute:chronic ratio) shows when you are ramping up too
> fast.
>
> **Your data, in one place.** Connect Health Connect to bring in steps,
> sleep, heart rate, HRV, weight, blood pressure, SpO2 and more from your
> watch, scale and other apps. Record runs with GPS. Track sleep sounds,
> meals, water, habits, mood, cycle and medicines.
>
> **Built for India.** Indian foods and medicines, air quality before you
> head out, and help lines that work here (112, 108, Tele-MANAS 14416).
>
> **Private by design.** You choose what the app may do with your data, one
> purpose at a time. Export everything or delete your account from Settings.
> Encrypted at rest.
>
> **Free.** Every feature is free. A daily limit applies to AI coach replies.
>
> AdapFit is a wellness app. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and
> is not a substitute for a doctor. In an emergency call 112.
