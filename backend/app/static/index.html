ACWR Zone Guide
Danger Zone (>1.5)
-15 recovery penalty. Deload mandatory. Overtraining risk.
Caution (1.3-1.5)
-5 recovery penalty. Reduce volume next session.
Sweet Spot (0.8-1.3)
No penalty. Optimal training zone. Progressive overload safe.
Under-Training (<0.8)
No penalty. Consider increasing training volume.
Recovery Score Formula
With HRV (wearable mode):
40% HRV + 35% Sleep + 25% Subjective + ACWR penalty
Without HRV (manual mode):
55% Sleep + 45% Subjective + ACWR penalty
HRV Z-Score: (today_hrv - 28d_mean) / 28d_std. Normalized 0-100.
