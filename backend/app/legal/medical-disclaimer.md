# AdapFit Health Disclaimer

Version 2026-09-29.

**AdapFit is not a doctor and not a medical device.** It tracks what you measure, compares it with your own history, and suggests general wellness steps. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and nothing in it replaces advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

- **In an emergency call 112** (ambulance 108). Do not wait for the app. Chest pain, stroke signs, severe bleeding, trouble breathing, and thoughts of harming yourself need help now. For mental health support call Tele-MANAS on 14416.
- **Medicines**: the app never tells you to start, stop or change a dose. Interaction checks list questions to ask your prescriber.
- **Readings** from phone cameras, wearables and questionnaires can be wrong. When a reading worries you, check it with a proper device or a professional.
- **Screening tools** such as risk questionnaires and skin photo measurements show whether something is worth getting checked. They cannot tell you that you have or do not have a condition.
- **AI replies** are generated by a language model and can be wrong. Emergency messages get fixed replies written in advance, not AI text.
- **Exercise**: check with a doctor before starting a new programme if you are pregnant, have a heart condition, diabetes, high blood pressure, recent surgery or injury, or are unsure. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.
- **Pregnancy and children**: pregnancy features are for tracking. Reduced baby movement, bleeding, severe headache or swelling need your maternity team the same day.

When the app says to see a professional, it names the kind and how soon. Follow that, and your own judgement, over any number on a screen.
