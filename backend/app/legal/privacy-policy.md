# AdapFit Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-29. Draft for legal review; items in [brackets] are to be completed.

AdapFit is run by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] ("we"). We are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

## What we collect

- **Account details**: email, username, display name, date of birth, password (stored only as a salted hash).
- **Health and fitness records you enter or sync**: workouts, daily check-ins, heart rate and HRV, sleep, meals and water, body measurements, cycle, fertility and pregnancy logs, medications, symptoms, mood and mental-health check-ins, medical ID, and the other trackers you choose to use.
- **Files you upload**: meal photos, skin photos, raw DNA files. A DNA file is read once for a fixed panel of variants and then discarded.
- **Location**, only when you ask for care nearby, air quality or a GPS route.
- **Parent or guardian details** for a user under 18: name, relationship, email, and the time consent was given.

We do not collect Aadhaar numbers, and we never sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## Why we use it, and your consent

Each purpose is asked for separately, and you can change any of them in **Settings, Privacy**:

| Purpose | Needed? | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Store and use my health and fitness data | Yes | Keeping your records, showing your history and personalising plans against your own baseline |
| AI features | Optional | Sending your message and the records it needs to Google Gemini or Groq to write coach replies, read meal photos and parse goals. Off means the app answers from built-in rules |
| Sharing with family and community | Optional | Family invitations, community posts, challenges and peer support |
| Product analytics | Optional, never under 18 | Anonymous usage counts to fix and improve the app |

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and takes effect at once. Withdrawing the first purpose stops the app from working for you; it does not delete your data until you ask for deletion.

When the policy changes in a way that affects a purpose, we ask again before continuing.

## Children

A user under 18 cannot give consent. At sign-up they name a parent or guardian, who receives an email and decides for them after declaring that they are the parent or lawful guardian and 18 or over. Nothing beyond the sign-up details is stored until then, and an account with no guardian decision after 7 days is deleted. For children we never use product analytics, behavioural tracking or targeted advertising.

## Who else sees your data

- **Service providers** that process data for us under contract: [HOSTING PROVIDER] for servers and the database; Google (Gemini) and Groq for AI features, only with your consent; [EMAIL PROVIDER] for account emails.
- **Public data services** that receive only the query needed: OpenStreetMap for care nearby, Open-Meteo for air quality and UV, the National Medical Commission register when you check a doctor's registration.
- **People you choose**: family members see only the categories you allow for each connection; community posts are visible to other users.
- **Authorities**, only where Indian law requires it.

Some providers process data outside India. We do not transfer data to any country the Government of India has restricted.

## How long we keep it

| Record | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Your account and every health record | Until you delete the account; erased 30 minutes after you ask, unless you cancel |
| Account unused for 3 years | Erased, after an email warning 48 hours before |
| Child account with no guardian decision | Erased after 7 days |
| Guardian consent link | 72 hours |
| Sign-in sessions | 30 days from last use |
| Consent history | As long as the account exists |
| Raw DNA file | Not kept; discarded after reading |
| Database backups | Up to [30] days, after which erased records are gone from backups too |
| Security logs | 1 year, as required by the Rules; they hold account ids, not health records |

## Your rights

- **Access and portability**: Settings, Data Export downloads everything we hold about you as one JSON file.
- **Correction**: edit or delete any record in the app, or write to us.
- **Erasure**: Settings, Delete Account. We ask for your password, sign you out, and erase everything 30 minutes later; sign back in within that time to cancel.
- **Withdraw consent**: Settings, Privacy.
- **Nominate** someone to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act: write to the Grievance Officer.
- **Complain**: to our Grievance Officer first; we reply within [7] days and resolve within 90 days. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Security and breaches

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are hashed, and each account can reach only its own records. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board without delay, with what happened, what it means for you and what you can do.
